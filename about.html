About

We would like to welcome you to the York St John Computer Science Society. We aim to be an inclusive society for anyone with a keen interest in computing.

Whether you are interested in games development, web design, programming or cyber security we would love to welcome you into our community. Join us for our weekly meetings on campus where you can meet others just like you, or come and join in on our socials.

Our meetings can involve programming challenges, hackathons, UI design, professional workshops or quizzes. We hope you can join us and have fun.
